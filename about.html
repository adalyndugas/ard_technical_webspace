About

I’m a technical writer with a background in rhetoric, design, and user-centered communication. My work focuses on making complex systems readable, ethical, and intuitive—especially in academic, engineering, and research contexts.

I approach documentation as both a technical and human practice: clear structure, thoughtful design, and respect for the reader’s cognitive load. My experience includes instructional content, visual rhetoric analysis, grant-adjacent documentation, and design-forward technical communication.

I’m currently completing graduate work in technical communication while collaborating with engineering teams on research-driven writing projects.
